Overview

India's leading B2B pharmaceutical marketplace connecting 500+ top pharma companies and thousands of verified buyers. Built with Flutter to deliver real-time lead alerts, verified inquiries, credit balance tracking, and seamless catalogue discovery.

Challenge

The legacy portal was desktop-heavy and transactional. Sellers struggled to track live buyer leads on mobile, manage package credits on the go, and respond quickly to high-value franchise inquiries.

Approach

Architected a robust cross-platform app using Clean Architecture with BLoC & Riverpod reactive state flow. Integrated Dio with custom interceptors for token refresh, built cached catalog feeds, and designed responsive mobile-first dashboards.

Outcome

Reduced lead response time by 45%, achieved 99.8% crash-free sessions across Android & iOS, and streamlined daily lead purchases for thousands of active distributors.

What mobile tech stack, libraries, and tools do you use?

My daily development toolkit includes Flutter 3.x, Dart 3.x, Android Studio, Kotlin, and Jetpack Compose. How do you ensure 60 FPS performance and mobile security?

I enforce strict 60/120 FPS standards with zero UI thread jank by using const widget constructors, RepaintBoundaries, and virtualized list pagination. How long does a mobile app project take?

Timelines depend on complexity and platform scope. Rapid MVP releases or specific feature module sprints typically take 2 to 4 weeks, while comprehensive enterprise mobile applications featuring custom design systems, complex offline sync, payment gateways, and dual-store releases typically span 6 to 12 weeks.
